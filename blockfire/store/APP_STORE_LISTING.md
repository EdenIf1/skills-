## Description (4000)
Cubefront is a fast, blocky first-person shooter you can jump into in seconds. No sign-up needed: play as a guest and your progress is saved online from the very first match.

BUILD COVER MID-FIGHT
Pinned down? Throw up a wall. Place dirt, wood, glass, sandbags, brick, concrete or steel blocks wherever you aim. Every block you build can be shot or blown up, even on the Indestructible map, so the battlefield keeps changing.

6V6 AND 12V12 BLOCK WAR
Elimination, Elimination Pro (one life a round), Search & Destroy (plant or defuse the bomb at site A or B), Kill the King (one giant king a side), Knives Only, Snipers Only, Indestructible and the giant 12v12 Frontline across nine battle maps: Old Town, Rail Yard, Outpost with its sniper houses, a farm full of animals, a Giant House, Moon Base, Deep Forest, burning Downtown and The Code. Every map plays every mode. Bots fill every empty spot at the difficulty you pick (easy, medium or hard), so a match is always ready.

9 PARTY MODES
Gun Game, Capture the Flag, Domination, Free-for-All, Infected, Hardpoint, Battle Royale, One in the Chamber and Build Battle.

PLAY WITH FRIENDS
Open a room and it shows up in the Rooms lobby for everyone, or lock it with a password for friends only. Join any open room in one tap, see who left and take their spot. Bots top up the rest.

HOLD THE HOUSE
Team up against waves of zombies in a three-storey safehouse. Board the windows, buy weapons off the walls, hit the mystery box, grab the gas mask upstairs, see your teammates' health at a glance, and hold out against brutes, venom spitters and packs of exploding hounds across four difficulty levels.

A FULL ARMORY
45 guns that unlock as you level up: rifles, SMGs, shotguns, LMGs, snipers, pistols (twin pistols too) and a rocket launcher, each with its own sound and labelled Rookie, Veteran or Elite. Or try your luck with Rookie, Veteran and Elite mystery boxes (bought with in-game GP, odds always shown). Five grenade types, three knives, an airstrike you aim from a tablet and a nuclear strike for a 15-kill streak. Find the secret weapons stash hidden somewhere on every map. Fit scopes, grips and extended mags, and see every attachment and camo in a live 3D preview before you buy.

RENT IT OR OWN IT
Not sure about a gun? Tap it and pick 1 day, 3 days, a week, a month or 90 days, or buy it forever, with every price and the end date shown before you pay. Skins, gear, camos and attachments work the same way. Changed your mind? Sell anything you own forever back for 70% of what you paid. One item is 25% off every day.

LOOT WITH SIX RARITIES
Open supply crates for camos, character skins, building blocks, titles, banners and kill effects, from Common to Mythic. Odds are always shown, duplicates turn into GP, and a pity counter guarantees an Epic or better within 30 crates.

MAKE IT YOURS
Gear that adds health, a shield or speed. Skins, camos, hit-effect colors, titles, banners and loadouts. Pick your callsign once; changing it later costs 500,000 GP. Move every touch button where you like it. Level up, earn GP, open a gift every day of the month, finish daily challenges and climb the leaderboards.

CLIMB THE RANKS
Turn on Ranked and climb from Bronze to Master against Hard bots. A new season starts every month, with GP for the best rank you reached, and the world top 100 is always one tap away. Daily and weekly challenges, a free monthly season pass, and events: double XP on the 1st and 3rd weekend of every month, plus a featured mode each week that pays +50% GP.

FRIENDS
Add friends by name, see who is online and join their game in one tap. Share your invite link: when a friend makes an account from it, you both get GP and a free crate.

Built for iPhone: smooth touch controls with a second fire button, aim assist, haptics, game controller support (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro), a Colorblind mode, music and directional sound, and full offline single-player.

## Export compliance
Uses only standard encryption provided by the operating system: HTTPS, WebRTC DTLS, and Web Crypto (ECDH P-256 key exchange + AES-GCM) for the end-to-end encrypted backup route between players, plus ECDSA P-256 to check the signature of live updates. No custom or proprietary algorithms. `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` is set in Info.plist. If App Store Connect asks: "uses encryption: yes, standard algorithms only, provided by Apple's OS" (exempt). Have this confirmed before the first submission if unsure.

## App Review notes (paste and fill in)
On first launch the game asks once for the year of birth (neutral screen). Choose a year that makes you 13 or older to test online features; under 13 the game stays offline (no account, no online rooms).
Cubefront works without an account: a guest account is created automatically and progress is saved online.
To test a full account: tap the player name (top right) → Account → "Create account", or "Sign in" with the test account below.
Test account: username ______ · password ______   (create it in the game first; do not commit it to the repository)
Multiplayer: tap "Invite friends" on one device; on a second device open Lobby → Rooms and tap Join (or type the room code). Or press Play for a match with bots. Search & Destroy: hold E / the PLANT button inside a site to plant, DEFUSE next to the bomb to defuse. Party modes: Home → ★ Party modes.
Safety: offensive usernames and names that impersonate the game team are refused (server-side); the game owner's GM badge is verified by the server; the owner can suspend reported accounts from online play (admin panel → Player messages → Ban); in a room, ⚑ reports a player to us, ⊘ blocks a player (Settings → Blocked players to undo) and the host's ✕ removes a player. Reports also go through Settings → Help & feedback → "Report a player".
Account deletion: player name → Account → Delete account (type DELETE). Works for guest accounts too. Web page for deletion requests: support.html#delete.
Building: in any mode except Zombies, tap BUILD to see where the block goes, then tap BUILD again to place it.
Friends: 👥 at the top. Each request and friend has ⊘ Block (declines or removes them and hides them from now on).
Invite rewards: Friends → Share my link. When a new account is made from that link, both players get 5,000 in-game GP and a free crate (in-game items only, checked on the server, at most 10 a month).
Ranked: Home → Ranked On. Leaderboards → Ranked shows the world top 100.
Game controllers: connect any Xbox, PlayStation or Switch Pro controller; the D-pad moves through menus, A selects, B goes back.
Game updates: the app is fully playable offline from the files inside the build. It can download signed fixes for this same version (1.8.x) only; new features always come as an App Store update.
Everything in the game is earned with in-game GP; there are no real-money purchases.
