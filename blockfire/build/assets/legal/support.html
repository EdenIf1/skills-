Common questions

How do I keep my progress? It is saved online automatically, even as a guest. To use it on another phone, open Profile → Account, pick a username and password, then log in with them on the other phone.

My friend cannot join my room. Keep the game open on screen while they join, and update both phones to the newest version. Settings → Test connection shows what your network allows.

How do I delete my account? Profile → Account → Delete account. Everything is removed from our servers at once.
