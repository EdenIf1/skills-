CUBEFRONT privacy policy

Effective 18 October 2026

Cubefront is a multiplayer block shooter. This page explains what the game stores about you, why, who else is involved, and how to delete it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

Who we are

Cubefront is made and run by an independent developer in Israel (“we”). We are responsible for the data described here. You can reach us from the game (Settings → Help & feedback) or from the support page; we answer within 30 days.

We do not collect your real name, email (unless you type one in Help), contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifiers. There are no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell data.

Playing online with others

Your network (IP) address is never shown to other players. Every multiplayer link between devices goes through a relay server, so the devices in a room only ever see the relay's address, never each other's. The services that help connect you are: the PeerJS matchmaking server, TURN relay servers (freestun.net, Metered Open Relay, or the game's own relay), and, as a backup route, our own relay on Supabase Realtime or public message relays (EMQX, HiveMQ). These services see your connection the way any web server does. Game traffic on the backup route is end-to-end encrypted, so those message relays cannot read it. Game data sent between players is your name, level, look, and your position and actions in the match, never your password or saved progress.

Who stores the data

Accounts, progress, Help messages and crash reports are stored with Supabase (database hosting) in Frankfurt, Germany. The game files are hosted on GitHub. The iPhone app checks GitHub for updated game files and downloads them; this sends no personal data. Players you block are saved with your progress so you do not meet them again. These providers process data only to run those services.

Why we may use it (legal basis)

We use your data to provide the game you asked for (keeping your account, progress and online play working), for our legitimate interest in keeping the game safe, fair and free of crashes (reports, suspensions, crash reports), and to answer you when you contact us.

Data outside your country

Our database is in the European Union (Frankfurt). Some connection services (the PeerJS matchmaking server, relay servers and public message relays) may run in other countries, including the United States. They only carry connection and encrypted game traffic, never your saved progress or password.

How long we keep it

We keep nothing longer than we need. Your account and progress are kept until you delete them (that is what lets you keep playing). Everything else is deleted automatically: Help messages, player reports and crash reports after 30 days, room tickets and invites after 2 days, fair play notes after 90 days, and a device's sign-in key after 30 days without use. Notification addresses are deleted when you turn notifications off or the browser stops accepting them. Suspensions last until they end or are lifted. Our safety copy of the database is replaced every day and holds at most the last 3 days.

Deleting your data

Open the game, go to Profile → Account → Delete account and type DELETE (guests too). Your account and all of its progress are deleted from our servers at once. Progress saved only on your device is removed when you clear the game's data or delete the app.

Children

Cubefront is not directed to children under 13. When a player says they are under 13, the game works offline only: no online account is made, no online rooms, and nothing about them is sent to our servers. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Israel's Privacy Protection Law), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data. Use the Help screen in the game or the support page; we answer within 30 days. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Israel, the Privacy Protection Authority; in the EU, the authority where you live).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
