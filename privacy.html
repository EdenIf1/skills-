CUBEFRONT privacy policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Cubefront is a multiplayer block shooter. This page explains what the game stores about you, why, who else is involved, and how to delete it. We keep it short and plain on purpose.

What we collect

DataWhyLinked to you
Your username (or a temporary guest name) and a passwordTo sign you in and keep your progress. Passwords are stored by our database provider in hashed form; we never see them.Yes
Game progress: level, XP, GP, unlocked weapons, skins, blocks, stats and match historyTo save your progress so it is there on any device.Yes
Messages you send from the in-game Help screen, and an optional contact you addTo answer you and fix problems.Yes, if you are signed in
Open rooms you host: your username, the room code, mode, map, number of players and whether it is locked (shown to other players in Rooms for as long as the room is open; untick "Listed in Rooms" to hide it). We do not collect your real name, email (unless you type one in Help), contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifiers. There are no ads, no tracking across other apps or websites, and we never sell data.

Playing online with others

Multiplayer rooms connect players' devices directly (peer-to-peer) or through relay services. Like any online game, the devices in a room can see each other's network (IP) addresses while you play together. The services that help connect you are: the PeerJS matchmaking server, public STUN/TURN relay servers (Google, Cloudflare, freestun.net, Metered Open Relay), and, when a direct connection is not possible, public message relays (EMQX, HiveMQ, Eclipse Mosquitto). Game data sent through them is your position and actions in the match, never your password or saved progress.

Who stores the data

Accounts, progress, Help messages and crash reports are stored with Supabase (database hosting) in Frankfurt, Germany. The game files are hosted on GitHub. These providers process data only to run those services.

How long we keep it

Your account and progress are kept until you delete them. Help messages and crash reports are kept for up to 12 months.

Deleting your data

Open the game, go to Profile → Account → Delete account and type DELETE. Your account and all of its progress are deleted from our servers at once. Progress saved only on your device is removed when you clear the game's data or delete the app.

Children

Cubefront is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us from the Help screen and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Israel's Privacy Protection Law), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data. Use the Help screen in the game; we answer within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the game.
